◆ Yes — TinyFish likely unlocks the dropped workflow. Brave blocks it by
  default; TinyFish does not.

  What you dropped and why:

  • Your live path is deliberately ephemeral:
    src/app/api/organizations/[organizationId]/market-research/live-preview/rout
    e.ts:77 reads fresh Brave results, returns them, stores nothing — no DB, no
    cache, no event.
  • UI says the same: “Live preview — not saved. Unverified leads, not
    evidence.”
  • Your durable path wants the opposite: ResearchRetrievedSource.excerptText in
    ports.ts, citation slots in report.ts, span-checked citations in
    claim-extraction.ts / claim-support-review.ts.
  • Brave standard Terms (updated 1 Sep 2026) forbid that:
    • No store, cache, or database of Search Results except transient storage
      for operation.
    • No redistribute / resell / sublicense results.
    • No use of results to train / evaluate AI models.
    • No retention after termination.
    • Source: Brave Search API Terms
       (https://api-dashboard.search.brave.com/terms-of-service)
  • Brave sells the exception separately as “Data with Storage Rights” — not in
    the $5/1k standard plan. Source: Brave Search API plans
     (https://brave.com/search/api/)

  TinyFish findings:

  • API returns what Market monitoring needs:
    position, site_name, title, snippet, url, plus publisher + date for
    domain_type=news, and authors, venue, year, cited_by_count, pdf url for
    domain_type=research_paper. Also purpose, location/language, recency_minutes
    , after_date/before_date, include/exclude_domains. Source: Search examples
     (https://docs.tinyfish.ai/search-api/examples), Search API
     (https://docs.tinyfish.ai/search-api), Reference
     (https://docs.tinyfish.ai/search-api/reference)
  • No search-specific storage ban in docs, reference, or pricing. Search and
    Fetch are free (Search: 30 req/min, 500/hour; Fetch: 150 urls/min,
    1,000/day). Source: Pricing (https://tinyfish.ai/pricing)
  • Terms distinction matters:
    • Restrictive clause is about “Site Content” (their website), not API
      outputs.
    • “Customer Data & Outputs” assigns Outputs rights to you. A search response
      to your query reads as an Output you own and can persist.
    • Source: TinyFish Terms (https://tinyfish.ai/terms) effective Aug 17, 2026
  • Think of it like renting vs owning a newspaper clipping: Brave lends you
    today’s paper but forbids a filing cabinet unless you buy the archive
    license. TinyFish hands you the clipping and says file it.

  Caveats before you build:

  • Get storage permission in writing from TinyFish (support / enterprise order
    form), since the permission is inferred from Outputs assignment, not an
    explicit “you may persist search results indefinitely.”
  • Third-party copyright still applies: store title + URL + short excerpt for
    citations, fetch full pages transiently — same 2,000-char excerpt discipline
    your Brave adapter already uses.
  • TinyFish licenses Customer Data to improve models with safeguards. For
    sensitive monitoring queries, ask for opt-out / DPA and enterprise terms
    (ISO 27001, SSO, audit logs offered). Also confirm rate-limit raise above
    30/min and SLA if daily monitoring + weekly synthesis scales.
  • Plan a small adapter swap: new TinyFish transport + schema map
    (snippet→excerptText, publisher/date, authors/venue), keep tenant checks,
    budget, kill-switch, span validation, and expiry/erasure paths unchanged.

  Verdict: Partial-to-Yes — TinyFish removes the ToS blocker and is richer and
  cheaper for citations, pending that one written storage confirmation and an
  enterprise privacy review.

    clearance.

  Success Criteria

  • A qualified organization can run daily monitoring and weekly synthesis that
    persist citation-backed sources and produce cited findings, competitor
    findings, limitations, and source inspection in the Insights and market tab.
  • An unqualified organization sees the existing safe disabled state; no
    credential, contract, or payload leaks into safe surfaces.
  • Every retained excerpt carries TinyFish qualification provenance and an
    agreed retain-until policy, and erasure renders sources unavailable with

  • Controlled canary passes on staging, tenant isolation is verified, and spec

  Context And Current Facts

  • Spec 022 keeps durable cited Market Evidence in scope: Cited Market Evidence
    with source, date, geography, support grade, freshness, and limitations; 26

  • Spec 022 section 4.2 out-of-scope forbids a general-purpose crawler and a
