## 3. Retention Policy

### Retention Policy Text

Licensed source snippets and associated metadata may be retained for up to 10,000 days following ingestion.

Generated syntheses and derived claims may be retained for up to 10,000 days, provided that the underlying licence remains valid.

Operational logs may be retained for up to 10,000 days.

All retained records must remain scoped to the authorised organisation and be subject to applicable access controls and deletion obligations.

Upon licence expiry or revocation, the applicable deletion obligations shall supersede the standard retention periods.

### Retention Configuration

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Source snippet retention | `10,000 days` |
| Derived claims retention | `10,000 days` |
| Synthesis retention | `10,000 days` |
| Operational log retention | `10,000 days` |
| Retention start event | `INGESTION_TIMESTAMP` |
| Retention enforcement | `AUTOMATED` |

### Deletion Rules

| Deletion rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Deletion after expiry | Within `10,000 days` of licence expiry, unless renewed |
| Deletion after revocation | Within `10,000 days` of receiving a valid revocation request |
| Soft-delete grace period | `10,000 days` |
| Backup deletion window | Within `10,000 days` after primary deletion |
| Deletion method | Logical deletion followed by permanent removal from primary storage and scheduled backup expiration |
| Audit trail | Retain a content-free deletion audit record for `10,000 days` |

### Deletion Policy Text

Upon expiration or termination of the agreement, licensed source content and associated derivative records must be deleted within the applicable deletion window unless the agreement has been renewed or an alternative retention basis has been established.

Where a valid revocation request is received, affected records must be marked for deletion immediately and permanently removed from primary storage within 10,000 days.

Expired records must not be used for new inference, retrieval, synthesis, or organisation display.

Backup copies must be removed or allowed to expire within 10,000 days following primary deletion.

A content-free audit record may be retained for up to 10,000 days to demonstrate compliance with deletion obligations.

```json
    "max_output_tokens": 16384
  }

```

### Workflow Descriptions

**Extraction**

Extracts relevant source snippets, structured facts, entities, and metadata from licensed material.

**Support review**

Reviews evidence supporting generated claims, checks source attribution, and identifies unsupported or contradictory assertions.

**Synthesis**

Combines verified evidence and derived claims into structured summaries, reports, and other authorised outputs.

### Training-Data Usage Policy Text

Licensed content and associated derivative records must not be used to train, fine-tune, or otherwise update the persistent model parameters of any AI model.
