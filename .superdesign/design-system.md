# Revenue Intelligence System — Onboarding Design System

AI Revenue OS is a calm, financially serious, multi-tenant operator cockpit. The onboarding user is an agency operator constructing trustworthy context for a client organization. Preserve the existing New York shadcn/Radix language, semantic CSS variables, compact-professional density, restrained borders/shadows, and Arial system typography. Do not invent gradients, decorative illustrations, consumer-app patterns, or unrelated colors.

The approved onboarding direction is a hybrid: a persistent section rail showing all ten sections and a focused work panel with a compact six-phase stepper. The active panel uses animated directional slide transitions and smooth dynamic height, but motion must be short, purposeful, and disabled or reduced under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Each step supports save/resume, unknown values, source/verification status, client follow-up requests, and clear completion.

Ten sections: business identity; branches and operations; products/services and restaurant menu; channels and digital presence; historical performance; customers and consent; brand assets and communication style; goals, budget, constraints, and approvals; integrations and data uploads; review and readiness.

Use shadcn/ui `Button`, `Card`, `Field`, `FieldGroup`, `Input`, `Textarea`, `Select`, `Checkbox`, `Progress`, `Badge`, `Alert`, `Empty`, `AlertDialog`, `Sidebar`, and `DropdownMenu`. Keep organization scope visible. AI extraction is a suggestion state and never silently becomes verified truth.

## Business Memory workspace extension

Business Memory is an authenticated, multi-tenant operator workspace for evidence-based retrieval and governed review. It is not a generic knowledge base: each result shows source, trust, freshness, sensitivity, and whether review is required. Keep the same shadcn/Radix language, system sans typography, neutral/emerald palette, dense desktop-first ergonomics, restrained borders, and reduced-motion support.

The approved direction is an operator console with a prominent full-width workspace search bar, a slim operational snapshot row, compact structured filters, trust-ranked result groups, and an optional review queue. The search default has Search, Timeline, Lessons, and Review tabs. "Inspect chain" opens a shadcn `Dialog`, with provenance, verification/freshness/sensitivity labels, source reference, supersession status, and safe inspect actions. Do not invent revenue metrics, generic dashboards, external fonts, gradients, or colour-only status.

## Unified Campaign Bundle extension

Campaigns are an organization-scoped operator control room, not a generic social scheduler. Preserve the existing fixed AppShell, floating organization sidebar, route breadcrumb, compact tabs, semantic status copy, neutral surfaces, and emerald primary actions. The experience must make the complete proposal understandable before approval: source and objective, three required creative directions (`Control`, `Evidence-led`, `Experimental`), visual asset, editable hook/caption/hashtags/tags/CTA, timing rationale and schedule, eligible organic/paid actions, exact capability restrictions, spend ceiling, measurement plan, visual attestation, immutable version diff, execution receipts, and business proof.

Use a desktop-first master-detail composition with calm professional density. Let the campaign artwork provide the visual energy; the application chrome stays restrained. A persistent bundle header carries lifecycle, version, source, approval expiry, and dry-run state. The main workspace gives the selected creative enough room to judge the image and post as a whole, while a compact direction switcher makes all three alternatives comparable without turning them into tiny dashboard cards. A right review rail groups generation profile, channel readiness, schedule, spend, measurement, and the final approval action. Focused copy/hashtag/schedule editing and prompt revision use sheets or dialogs and always preview the material version change before saving.

The three generation profiles are explicit operator controls: `Brand restricted`, `Brand guided`, and `Full visual freedom`. Experimental work may be visually surprising, but factual claims, offers, legal/safety constraints, provider limits, consent, and approved spend remain immovable. Blocked placements remain visible with a plain-language reason and recovery action; unavailable never looks enabled. Approval requires the visual attestation and binds the exact version/digest/action set/spend ceiling. After approval, the same detail route becomes an execution-and-proof timeline with scheduled actions, receipts, reconciled provider state, measurement window, evidence grade, limitations, and campaign-scoped learning proposal.

Mobile is a review surface, not a compressed desktop editor: one creative at a time, thumb-reachable direction switching, readable caption and hashtags, expandable readiness/spend/measurement sections, and a sticky attestation-plus-approve area. Telegram review must visually match this contract while remaining operator control only.

Use only the repo's system sans typography, semantic CSS variables, neutral/emerald chart ramp, compact shadcn `Card`, `Tabs`, `Badge`, `Alert`, `Button`, `Dialog`, `Sheet`, `Field`, `Select`, `Textarea`, `ToggleGroup`, `Progress`, and `ScrollArea` patterns. No gradients, glassmorphism, decorative serif type, neon AI styling, invented provider capability, fabricated business result, or colour-only state.

## Organization Overview extension

The organization Overview is the shared front door for non-technical clients, operators, and administrators. It must answer three questions in the first viewport: "Are we on track?", "What changed?", and "What needs attention?" Lead with an evidence-backed AI briefing in plain business language, followed by a small outcome snapshot and a prioritized attention queue. Every generated conclusion links to its evidence, freshness, and confidence state; missing evidence remains visible and is never converted into a confident claim.

The default experience is a client-readable briefing, not an editing form or a generic analytics grid. Preserve operator power through a clearly separated "Manage organization" surface for Digital Twin fields, policies, lifecycle controls, and audit history. Role and permission states must remain understandable: a client or read-only member can inspect evidence and outcomes without seeing unusable mutation controls, while operators and administrators can reach setup and recovery actions without leaving organization scope.

Compose the main story from real module read models. Channel economics shows gross revenue and contribution margin by channel only when the ledger permits the figure, always carrying its completeness grade; an indicative value is worded as an upper bound and never presented as profit. Integration health shows healthy, stale, degraded, or blocked sources and the next recovery action, but never treats a visible provider as connected or authorized. Recent campaigns shows the latest three ideas as compact expandable rows with lifecycle, objective, source, channels, spend ceiling, blockers, and a review link. Until production campaign persistence exists, sample campaigns must carry one unmistakable preview label and must never imply publication or measured business results.

Use restrained visual hooks: compact emerald chart marks, comparison bars, sparklines only where a real time series exists, progressive disclosure, meaningful icons, and short directional transitions. Keep the application chrome neutral and let information hierarchy create energy. Use no invented KPI, fake trend, decorative AI orb, chat-first layout, gradient, glass effect, or dense wall of equal-weight cards. Mobile prioritizes the briefing, attention queue, and one module summary at a time; management controls remain secondary and touch-friendly.

The approved Overview composition is the Performance and Readiness Intelligence Cockpit. Place a full-width six-section Digital Twin readiness strip beneath organization context, then use an 8/4 desktop grid: Strategic Briefing, a tabbed shadcn Channel Economics chart, progressive Current Digital Twin Data, and three preview Campaign ideas in the primary column; Action Required, enabled Integration Health, and Recent Activity in the rail. The economics tabs are Trend, Channel comparison, and Data trust. Management forms remain collapsed and role-gated below the client-readable story.
