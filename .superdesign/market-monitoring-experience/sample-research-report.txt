The short version

Make the event offer easy to understand, and check that the team can deliver it well. The
useful opportunity is a clear family occasion, backed by reliable service.


Research question

Understand nearby offers and customer expectations so we can plan a useful National Day
promotion.


Family offers appear in the local comparison

The sampled public menu includes a family offer. Its visibility does not tell us how many
customers buy it. [S1]


Feedback includes praise and delivery concerns

The illustrative review sample contains positive service comments and mentions of delays.
It is not a representative customer survey. [S2]


Your business evidence covers an earlier period

The available internal channel evidence is from August, while the external market
observations are from September. [S3]


Evidence gaps

This research does not establish competitor sales or profit. Operating capacity is not
confirmed, and the internal business evidence covers August.


Competitors and their offers

Rival Kitchen

Family offer is easy to find in its public menu. [S1]

Compare the bundle contents and occasion, not just the headline price.


Neighbour Table

Sample feedback mentions both friendly service and delivery delays. [S2]

Investigate service consistency as a possible point of difference.

Speculative estimate

Rival Kitchen | Monthly sales scenario: AED 45,000-70,000 per month

Speculative estimate, not reported revenue. These are assumed trading volumes, not measured
competitor orders.

Scenario only: assume 40-60 orders per day, an average order value of AED 38 and 30 trading
days. That gives AED 45,600-68,400, rounded to a broad range. The order volume is an
analyst assumption; reviews and visible offers do not establish it. This is sales before
costs, not profit.


The local opportunity

A family-focused event offer could be worth exploring if the bundle is clear and the team
has enough capacity. Start with the location’s existing customer occasion and service
constraints.


What to check before committing

Customer occasion: Make the intended customer and the reason to buy explicit.

Operating capacity: Confirm available items, preparation limits and fulfilment coverage at
this location.

Commercial fit: Check the proposed offer against costs and the organization’s goals before
approving a campaign.

Potential opportunity, not a promised result. The report proposes what to investigate; it
does not claim that a campaign has earned revenue or profit.


Draft advice for review

Draft one clear family bundle

Use the public offer comparison to identify a clear occasion and explain what the customer
receives. [S1] [S4]

Next step: Confirm the items, availability and margin before making it a campaign proposal.


Confirm service capacity before promoting

A promotion is only useful if the location can fulfil the extra demand reliably. [S2] [S3]

Next step: Review preparation times, cancellation reasons and the team’s capacity for the
chosen period.
