# ADR 0006: Use Industry Packs

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

Keep core platform entities and services industry-neutral. Add domain-specific onboarding sections, schemas, metrics, signals, workers, playbooks, and UI extensions through versioned Industry Packs.

## Consequences

- The Restaurant Pack can move quickly without permanently coupling the core to hospitality.
- Pack contracts must be explicit.
- The second industry will test whether the abstraction is real.
