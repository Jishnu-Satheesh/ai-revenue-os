# ADR 0007: Use Risk-Based Human Approvals

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

Classify actions by risk and require human approval proportional to financial, public, legal, privacy, brand, and reversibility impact.

## Consequences

- The two-person team avoids approval fatigue.
- Sensitive actions remain governed.
- Policies must be deterministic and versioned.
- Autonomy can increase gradually using evidence rather than trust alone.
