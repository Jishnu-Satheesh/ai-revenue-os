# Dubai Restaurant Pilot

## Current understanding

- The client operates a food-service restaurant in Dubai.
- Cuisine is multi-cuisine with a likely Indian focus.
- The business has operated offline for some time.
- It has not previously run structured Meta Ads or Google Ads.
- It is present on delivery marketplaces reportedly including Talabat, noon, and "DoorDash".
- The agency operates remotely from Kerala, India.
- This client is the pilot for a repeatable Dubai hospitality offering.

## Facts requiring confirmation

- Legal business name and branch count.
- Exact addresses and map listings.
- Correct marketplace providers and merchant account identities.
- Whether "DoorDash" means DoorDash, Deliveroo, or another service.
- POS system and availability of historical exports.
- Menu versions by branch and channel.
- Item-level food and packaging cost.
- Marketplace commission and promotion funding.
- Historical sales, orders, discounts, refunds, and preparation time.
- Existing website, domain, Google Business Profile, social accounts, and analytics.
- Customer database and communication consent.
- Operational capacity by daypart.
- Monthly test budget and approval thresholds.

## Pilot onboarding data checklist

### Business and branch

- Name, description, cuisine, service modes, languages.
- Address, geo-coordinates, phone, WhatsApp, hours, delivery radius.
- Seating and kitchen capacity.
- Busy and underutilized periods.

### Menu

- Categories, items, descriptions, prices, modifiers, photos.
- Costs or margin estimates.
- Best sellers, strategic items, unavailable items, preparation time.
- Channel-specific listings and promotions.

### Performance

- Prefer 90 to 180 days of order and sales exports when available.
- Marketplace impressions, views, conversion, cancellations, refunds, ratings.
- Offline POS sales and daypart patterns.

### Brand and marketing

- Logo, brand colors, fonts, photo library, tone, prohibited claims.
- Existing social content.
- Offer history.
- Target neighborhoods and customer segments.

### Access and governance

- Account ownership.
- Secure access method.
- Test budget.
- Approval contacts.
- Allowed and prohibited actions.
- Messaging consent and retention rules.

## Initial pilot sequence

1. Build Digital Twin and Readiness Score.
2. Normalize menu and marketplace listings.
3. Establish baseline and contribution-margin model.
4. Fix local discovery, listing, and tracking gaps.
5. Launch one low-risk listing experiment.
6. Launch review-response workflow.
7. Build first-party capture and retention foundation.
8. Launch a small paid-media experiment only after tracking and landing destinations are ready.

## Pilot success criteria

- Complete and trustworthy business dataset.
- At least one measured revenue or conversion improvement.
- A repeatable onboarding process.
- A reusable restaurant playbook.
- A client-facing decision timeline and report.
- Evidence that the agency can onboard the next Dubai restaurant faster.
