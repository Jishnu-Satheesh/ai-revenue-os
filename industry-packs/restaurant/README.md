# Restaurant Industry Pack

## Purpose

The Restaurant Industry Pack extends the platform with restaurant-specific entities, signals, playbooks, workers, integrations, and evaluation methods.

## Target outcomes

- More profitable dine-in and takeaway customers.
- More profitable marketplace orders.
- Higher repeat-order rate.
- Better local discovery and reputation.
- Improved menu conversion and contribution margin.
- More first-party customer relationships.
- Catering, office meal, and group-order leads.

## Initial workers

- Restaurant Growth Intelligence Worker.
- Menu Intelligence Worker.
- Marketplace Performance Worker.
- Reputation Worker.
- Retention and WhatsApp Worker.
- Local Discovery Worker.
- Campaign Experiment Worker.

## Initial playbooks

- Menu completeness and data-quality remediation.
- Item photo and description optimization.
- Low-conversion high-margin item test.
- Marketplace availability and stockout prevention.
- Review response and service-recovery follow-up.
- Google Business Profile optimization.
- First-party QR and loyalty capture.
- Lapsed-customer win-back.
- Office lunch and catering lead generation.
- Controlled Meta and Google campaign experiments.

## Important constraint

The pack must distinguish between gross sales and profitable sales. Marketplace commissions, discounts, refunds, food cost, packaging, and advertising cost materially affect value.
