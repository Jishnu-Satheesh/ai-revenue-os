# Restaurant Revenue Playbooks

## Priority tier 1 - Foundation and fast evidence

### Menu data quality and profitability map

**Problem:** The business cannot make good promotion decisions without normalized prices, costs, listings, and availability.

**Action:** Import every menu and marketplace listing, match items, flag discrepancies, estimate missing margins, and request verification.

**Revenue effect:** Prevents unprofitable promotions and identifies high-margin candidates.

### Marketplace listing conversion audit

**Problem:** Poor photos, titles, descriptions, category placement, price inconsistency, or unavailable items reduce conversion.

**Action:** Score listing quality and propose controlled improvements.

**KPIs:** Listing conversion, item orders, contribution margin, refunds, preparation time.

### Review response and service recovery

**Problem:** Negative reviews reduce local conversion and hide operational issues.

**Action:** Classify reviews, draft brand-safe responses, escalate severe complaints, and track recurring topics.

**KPIs:** Rating, review response time, issue recurrence, recovered customers where trackable.

### Google Business Profile local discovery

**Problem:** Offline restaurant discovery often begins with local search and maps.

**Action:** Audit profile completeness, categories, menu links, photos, posts, Q&A, tracking links, and review velocity.

**KPIs:** Calls, direction requests, website or menu clicks, tracked offer redemptions.

## Priority tier 2 - Retention and first-party growth

### QR customer capture

Offer a consented benefit through receipts, table tents, packaging, or checkout QR codes. Capture language, branch, and preference with minimal friction.

### Lapsed-customer win-back

Segment consented customers by recency, frequency, value, preferences, and margin. Send approved WhatsApp templates with controlled offers and holdouts.

### Post-purchase follow-up

Request feedback, recommend relevant items, and recover poor experiences without excessive messaging.

## Priority tier 3 - Demand generation

### Office lunch and catering lead engine

Identify nearby offices and communities, create appropriate packages, capture qualified leads, and manage follow-up. Human review is required before outbound outreach at scale.

### Paid-media experiment engine

After tracking readiness, create small-budget Meta or Google experiments with explicit hypotheses, geographic scope, audience, creative variants, landing destination, and stop rules.

### Marketplace promotion optimizer

Evaluate provider-funded versus merchant-funded discounts, commission, margin, operational capacity, and incrementality. Never optimize solely for gross order count.

## Measurement rule

Each playbook must define baseline, primary metric, guardrails, attribution method, and a measurement window before execution.
