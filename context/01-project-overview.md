# Project Overview

## Problem

Small and medium businesses often have fragmented data, limited marketing expertise, weak measurement, and no disciplined experimentation loop. Agencies can perform manual marketing work, but manual delivery does not scale for a two-person team and does not compound learning across clients.

The pilot restaurant wants more offline customers and more delivery orders. However, it may lack:

- Clean menu and margin data.
- Centralized performance reporting.
- First-party customer data.
- Advertising accounts and conversion tracking.
- Repeat-purchase automation.
- Local discovery optimization.
- A method to distinguish profitable growth from discount-driven vanity revenue.

## Users

### Agency owner / technical administrator

Creates organizations, configures integrations, reviews system health, governs AI actions, and develops new capabilities.

### Agency sales and account operator

Onboards clients, sets goals, collects missing information, reviews opportunities, communicates results, and manages relationships.

### Client owner or manager

Provides business context, approves sensitive actions, sees outcomes, and resolves operational constraints.

### Specialist reviewer

Optional future role for paid media, creative, legal, finance, or operations review.

## Core jobs to be done

- Onboard a business remotely and construct a trustworthy Digital Twin.
- Connect available data sources without making integrations a prerequisite for value.
- Identify missing data that blocks automation.
- Convert business goals into measurable outcome targets.
- Detect revenue opportunities and rank them by expected value, confidence, effort, cost, and risk.
- Execute low-risk actions and request approval for high-impact actions.
- Measure results and improve playbooks over time.
- Replicate the system across industries and clients.

## V1 product boundary

V1 should support:

- Multi-tenant organizations and branches.
- Guided onboarding.
- Restaurant menu ingestion and validation.
- Goal, constraint, budget, and approval configuration.
- Integrations and manual data uploads.
- Business Memory.
- Opportunity recommendations.
- Approval workflows.
- Trigger.dev execution runs.
- Audit and outcome timelines.

V1 should not promise:

- Fully autonomous advertising optimization without sufficient conversion data.
- Direct API control of every delivery marketplace.
- Guaranteed revenue results.
- Perfect attribution across offline and third-party channels.
- A universal digital twin for every industry on day one.

## Operating assumptions

- The team is initially two people: one senior software/AI engineer and one sales-focused teammate.
- Speed is critical, but quality, security, and architecture cannot be sacrificed.
- AI coding agents will perform significant implementation work from precise specifications.
- Features should be sliced so a production-complete unit can often be delivered in hours or a day.
